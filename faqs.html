Common Inquiries

Navigating the frontier of neuro-optimization. Find answers to our most frequently asked questions regarding clarity, recovery, and scientific methodology.

What exactly is Neurotherapy?

Neurotherapy, specifically Lajpatrai Mehra Neurotherapy (LMNT), is a non-invasive, drug-free healing methodology developed by Dr. Lajpatrai Mehra. It works on the principle that the human body is a self-healing organism governed by the nervous system. By applying precise manual pressure to specific nerve points, we facilitate the body's own healing processes — correcting faulty nerve signals, restoring glandular function, and reactivating the internal chemical factories responsible for cellular health. It combines the wisdom of traditional Indian medicine with modern anatomical science.

Is it painful?

No, Neurotherapy is generally not painful. The treatment involves skilled manual pressure applied to specific nerve points, which most patients describe as a "firm but comfortable" sensation. In cases where there is significant nerve inflammation, you may feel mild tenderness during the first few sessions — this typically resolves as the treatment progresses and inflammation reduces. Runu Garg calibrates the pressure to each patient's individual sensitivity and condition. There are no needles, no electrical stimulation, and no invasive procedures of any kind.

Do I need to stop my current medication?

No. You should never stop any prescribed medication without consulting the doctor who prescribed it. Neurotherapy works alongside your existing treatment — it does not conflict with pharmaceutical interventions. As your body begins to heal and regulate itself through Neurotherapy, your prescribing doctor may naturally adjust your medication dosage based on improved clinical markers. Many patients find their insulin requirements or blood pressure medication doses reduce over time, but this is always managed in consultation with their physician. We encourage transparent communication with all members of your healthcare team.
